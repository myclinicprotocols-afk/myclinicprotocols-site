FAQ

Clear from the start.

Is this guaranteed to be compliant in my state?

No. State-specific considerations can be incorporated, but final legal and clinical compliance depends on your practice circumstances and should be reviewed by a qualified medical professional and, where appropriate, legal counsel.

Can you customize for RNs, NPs, PAs and physicians?

Yes. Provider role is part of the intake and helps us structure the document around the practice workflow.

Will I receive an editable file?

Yes. The intended MVP delivery includes an editable Word version and a clean PDF version.

Can I add my clinic logo?

Yes. Branded customization can include your clinic name, contact information and logo.

Can I order more than one protocol?

Yes. Select as many treatments as your clinic needs. Founding bundle pricing is available for packages of 3, 5, and 10 treatments.

What is included in the Complete Treatment Package?

It includes the customized protocol plus all applicable consent, intake, treatment-record, aftercare, checklist, and emergency-guidance documents for the selected treatment.

How do revisions work?

Every order includes two consolidated revision rounds submitted within 14 days. A new treatment, state, device, or substantially different formula may require a new order.
