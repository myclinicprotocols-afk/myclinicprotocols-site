Frequently asked questions

Can I order more than one protocol?

Yes. Select as many treatments as your clinic needs. Bundle pricing is available for groups of 3, 5, and 10 treatments at the same package level.

Can you use our clinic’s medications and equipment?

Yes. The intake asks for applicable products, formulations, concentrations, device models, treatment settings, techniques, and workflow preferences. Clinical decisions still require qualified provider review.

Do I receive editable files?

Yes. Delivery includes an editable Word version and a polished PDF version.

What if we want changes to the first draft?

Return consolidated comments and recommendations. Up to two consolidated revision rounds are included when submitted within the stated review period.

Is the package guaranteed to be compliant in every state?

No. State considerations can be addressed during customization, but final compliance depends on the clinic’s circumstances and current law. Qualified clinical and legal review remains necessary.

Important: MyClinicProtocols provides customizable clinical documentation resources. The materials support—but do not replace—independent clinical judgment, legal advice, licensing and supervision requirements, manufacturer instructions, staff training, or applicable federal, state, and local law.
