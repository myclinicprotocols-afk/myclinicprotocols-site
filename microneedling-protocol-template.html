Frequently asked questions

Is this an instant generic download?

No. You complete one intake so the draft can be organized around your clinic, device, state, providers, workflow, requested documents, and branding.

Will I receive an editable file?

Yes. The final delivery includes an editable Word file and a polished PDF.

How long does preparation take?

Standard preparation is approximately 3–5 business days after the required clinic information and payment are received.

Can one protocol cover standard and RF microneedling?

They may share some policies, but device, energy, risk, training, treatment-record, and complication requirements should remain clearly distinguished.

Can PRP, serums, or topical products be included?

Tell us exactly what your clinic uses. Any product used with microneedling requires review of intended use, product status, evidence, infection risk, authorization, consent, and documentation.

Who can perform microneedling?

That depends on the state, depth, device, energy, medications or products used, professional license, supervision, and other facts. The final workflow requires clinic-specific clinical and, when needed, legal review.
