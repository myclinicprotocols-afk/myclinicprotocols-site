Editorial Standards & Clinical Review

How MyClinicProtocols researches, prepares, quality-checks, updates, and presents educational clinical documentation resources for aesthetic and wellness practices.

Our purpose

MyClinicProtocols publishes practical educational resources and prepares customizable documentation packages for aesthetic, wellness, and weight-management practices. The goal is to help clinics organize protocols, standing orders, consent forms, treatment records, SOPs, safety materials, and related documentation in a clear and usable format.

Transparency matters.

Our educational content and customized materials are designed to support qualified professional review—not replace medical judgment, legal advice, professional licensing requirements, or state-specific regulatory analysis.

Source standards

When a topic depends on clinical, regulatory, device, workplace-safety, or public-health information, we prioritize primary and authoritative sources. Depending on the topic, these may include:

Government sources

FDA, CDC, OSHA, HHS, state professional boards, statutes, regulations, and other official government publications.

Manufacturer information

Current device instructions, product labeling, prescribing information, safety communications, and other manufacturer documentation when relevant.

Professional guidance

Recognized professional organizations and specialty guidance when useful for context, training, or practice considerations.

Clinic-specific facts

The purchasing clinic's actual state, provider roles, products, devices, techniques, workflow, forms, and requested customization.

How materials are prepared

Educational pages are structured around a defined search or clinical-documentation question, reviewed for clarity and internal consistency, and linked to relevant supporting resources. Customized customer packages are prepared around the information supplied by the clinic rather than simply inserting a clinic name into a generic document.

MyClinicProtocols materials are prepared and quality-checked by a registered nurse with extensive experience supporting U.S. healthcare, aesthetic, and wellness practices. Quality review focuses on completeness, consistency, professional presentation, alignment across documents, and identification of areas that still require qualified clinical or legal approval.

Clinical review and approval

Final clinical approval remains with the purchasing clinic's appropriately qualified medical director, supervising provider, prescriber, or other authorized clinician. A protocol template or customized package does not expand scope of practice, create prescriptive authority, establish delegation, or substitute for any approval required by applicable law or professional regulation.

Where a resource says that a document was reviewed by a specific physician or other qualified professional, that statement should be used only when that review actually occurred. MyClinicProtocols does not represent every published page or customer file as individually physician-reviewed.

How we update content

Resources may be updated when there are meaningful changes in law, board guidance, FDA safety information, manufacturer instructions, products, devices, evidence, or common clinic workflows. Pages display an updated date where appropriate so readers can understand the freshness of the material.

Corrections and feedback

If a factual, sourcing, formatting, or consistency issue is identified, the material should be reviewed and corrected. Substantive changes may also trigger updates to related pages so the resource library remains internally consistent.

Important limitations

MyClinicProtocols does not provide legal advice and does not make final state-specific scope-of-practice determinations for a clinic. Educational content is not a substitute for patient-specific medical decision-making. Clinics remain responsible for confirming current requirements, maintaining appropriately licensed and trained personnel, and obtaining any required legal, medical-director, supervisory, or regulatory review before implementation.
